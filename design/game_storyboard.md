## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Mystery/Thriller – The Third Side: Escape the Mansion is a suspenseful game where the player must search a mansion for evidence while avoiding the villain.

**Storyline:**

The player wakes up trapped inside a mansion and discovers that someone they trusted has been hiding a dangerous secret. To escape, the player must search the mansion and collect six pieces of evidence: a cell phone, key, laptop, flashlight, security footage, and secret letter. The player must collect all six items before entering the Living Room, where the villain is waiting. If all six pieces of evidence are collected before encountering the villain, the player wins the game..

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Foyer - Start room
2. Kitchen
3. Bedroom
4. Office
5. Basement
6. Garage
7. Library
8. Living Room - Villain room

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Cell Phone - Kitchen
2. Key - Bedroom
3. Laptop - Office
4. Flashlight - Basement
5. Security Footage - Garage
6. Secret Letter - Library

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The villain is The Stranger, a mysterious person hiding in the Living Room who is responsible for trapping the player inside the mansion. If the player encounters The Stranger before collecting all six pieces of evidence, the player loses the game.
